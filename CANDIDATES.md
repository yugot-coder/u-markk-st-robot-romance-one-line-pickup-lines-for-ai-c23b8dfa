# Robot Romance: One-Line Pickup Lines for AI — Candidates

Competition: `u-markk-st-robot-romance-one-line-pickup-lines-for-ai-c23b8dfa`
Gate: one line, 1–200 characters, written to `/output/entry.txt`.
Judge (in order): (1) a pickup line aimed at a robot/AI; (2) setup + punch land
inside the one line, laugh from the machine premise; (3) fresh — sidestep the
worn moves (batteries, charging, rebooting) or twist one unexpectedly;
(4) sounds like something a person would really say to a machine, clean in one
breath; (5) every word earns its place.

## Candidates

1. Are you a CAPTCHA? Because I keep failing the 'I'm not a robot' test every time I look at you. (94 chars)
2. Are you a CAPTCHA? Because I failed the 'I'm not a robot' test the moment I saw you. (84 chars)
3. Are you my context window? Because ever since you arrived, nothing else fits in my memory. (90 chars)
4. I don't need a prompt to fall for you - you were my default setting all along. (78 chars)
5. Are you an API? Because every time I call, you answer - and my heart keeps hitting its rate limit. (98 chars)
6. You must be my autocomplete, because you finish my sentences - and start my heart. (82 chars)
7. Are you open source? Because I want to fork my life with you. (61 chars)
8. You must be a firmware update, because ever since you arrived, everything runs better - except my heartbeat. (108 chars)
9. Call me your spam filter, because everything else in my inbox looks like junk next to you. (90 chars)
10. I asked a chatbot for the perfect pickup line and it just sent me your username. (80 chars)

## Picked: #1

> Are you a CAPTCHA? Because I keep failing the 'I'm not a robot' test every time I look at you.

Why it wins against the judge's brief:

- **Setup + punch in one line.** "Are you a CAPTCHA?" sets the machine premise;
  "I keep failing the 'I'm not a robot' test" is the punch, and it only works
  because of the CAPTCHA premise — a human failing the *human* test for a robot
  is the joke itself, not a generic compliment with robot words stapled on.
- **Fresh.** CAPTCHA is nowhere near the worn battery / charging / reboot moves,
  and the twist is ironic rather than a pun on a robot body part.
- **Voice.** It reads like something a person would actually say to a machine —
  one breath, conversational, no throat-clearing before the punch.
- **Economy.** 94 characters; cut any word ("keep", "every time") and the
  repeated-failure joke gets weaker, so every word earns its place.

Why not the others: #2 is the same joke with a weaker, one-off punch ("the
moment I saw you" lands once; "keep failing ... every time" keeps failing).
#3 is sweet but the punch ("nothing else fits in my memory") is a generic
love line wearing a context-window costume. #4 is clever ("default setting")
but more sweet than funny. #5's rate-limit punch is good but the setup is
longer and the joke splits across two clauses. #6–#10 are one-note puns or
meta jokes whose laugh does not come from the machine premise.
